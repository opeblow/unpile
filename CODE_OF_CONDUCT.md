# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Unpile to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. Many people who use and build Unpile live with ADHD, anxiety, burnout or disability. Be kind about that.

## Expected behaviour

- Be patient and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Use plain, welcoming language. Explain jargon.
- Respect people’s time, energy and boundaries.
- Accept that “I can’t do this right now” is a valid answer.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Mocking someone’s productivity, disability or mental health.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone’s private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that break this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub (Security tab → “Report a vulnerability” works for private reports) or by the contact listed on the maintainer’s profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
